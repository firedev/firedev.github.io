UNPOS

Team Lead · Architecture · Full Stack

Cloud POS and warehouse platform built for a national pharmacy network.

UNPOS automated store and warehouse operations for Sber EApteka: checkout, returns, stock, consignments, pharmaceutical labeling, reporting, fiscal hardware, and ERP synchronization.

The architecture used schema-based multitenancy and isolated slices so deployments, experiments, and load could scale with the network. The platform integrated 1C, MDLP, OMS/TMS, loyalty, warehouse robots, scanners, customer displays, and fiscal devices.

Stack

Ruby on Rails · Hotwire · PostgreSQL · Redis · Sidekiq · Docker · RabbitMQ
